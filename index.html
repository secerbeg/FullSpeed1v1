Full-Speed Agility Session

A high-speed session that starts with pogo jumps, moves through the 5 shared agility videos, and finishes with a competitive 2-player 1v1 activity. Built for fast feet, sharp change of direction, and full-speed effort.

6 main activities + 1v1 finisher Each activity: 3 reps Finish: 11 min 1v1 block Focus: quick feet, acceleration, COD

Session standards

  • Every rep should be fast and sharp.
  • Recover enough so the next rep still looks explosive.
  • Stay low through the cuts and drive out of the plant foot.
  • Eyes up, arms active, and finish through the line.
  • For the 1v1 block, compete hard but keep the work organized.

Session Overview

Run the session in order. The jump work prepares the ankles and feet, then the training videos progress into sharper lateral movement, sprint transitions and curved acceleration.

How to run it

  • Activities 1-6: complete 3 quality reps each.
  • Rest: use about 30-45 seconds between reps and 60 seconds when athletes need extra recovery.
  • Intensity: this is a full-speed session, so keep the efforts explosive.
  • Coaching priority: posture first, then foot speed, then acceleration out of the movement.

Main coaching cues

  • Stay on the balls of the feet.
  • Arms drive fast and help the body turn.
  • Lower the hips before the cut.
  • Push the ground away on the first step.
  • Accelerate through the finish, not just to it.

Main Activities

Use the visuals to help the athlete understand what each drill should look like. The videos are embedded directly from the files you shared.

Activity 1 - Warm-Up

Pogo Jump Progression

  • Two-foot pogos: 12 jumps x 3 sets
  • Right-foot pogos: 10 jumps x 2 sets
  • Left-foot pogos: 10 jumps x 2 sets
  • Side-to-side jumps: 10 jumps x 2 sets

Coaching cues: stay tall, keep the jumps low, land quietly, and get off the ground quickly.

Purpose: prepare the feet, ankles and calves for the faster agility work that follows.
Activity 2 - Video 1

Hurdle Quickness

  • Reps: 3 runs
  • Goal: move the feet quickly while staying organized over the hurdles
  • Focus: fast steps, balance, and quick exit after the last hurdle

Coaching cues: lift the feet just enough, keep the torso stable, and accelerate immediately once clear.

Activity 3 - Video 2

Lateral Quick Feet

  • Reps: 3 reps
  • Goal: teach fast side-to-side foot action under control
  • Focus: stay low, keep the feet active, and avoid heavy contacts

Coaching cues: quick feet, hips level, and keep the body centered instead of leaning too far side to side.

Activity 4 - Video 3

Lateral Shuffle to Sprint

  • Reps: 3 reps each way if space allows
  • Goal: transition from lateral movement into forward acceleration
  • Focus: clean plant and powerful first 2 steps out of the shuffle

Coaching cues: stay low in the shuffle, load the outside foot, then explode into the sprint without popping up.

Activity 5 - Video 4

Sprint - Shuffle - Sprint

  • Reps: 3 runs
  • Goal: sprint, organize the feet, shuffle under control, then re-accelerate
  • Focus: body control in transition and an explosive restart

Coaching cues: brake under control, keep the feet under the hips, and push hard into the second sprint.

Activity 6 - Video 5

Curved Sprint

  • Reps: 3 reps each side
  • Goal: accelerate while running a curve and keeping control of the body line
  • Focus: forward lean, body tilt into the curve, and speed out of the final step

Coaching cues: lean slightly into the curve, pump the arms, and keep sprinting through the line instead of slowing early.

Competitive Finisher - 2-Player 1v1

Finish by applying the speed work in a live soccer action. The goal is to attack space, react under pressure and compete at full speed.

Final activity

2-Player 1v1 Soccer Activity

  • Format: one attacker, one defender, attack the goal at speed
  • Coaching focus: quick first step, speed change, body feint, protect the ball, recover fast on defense
  • Intensity: competitive and game-like

Simple setup: attacker starts with the ball, defender closes space, play is live to goal. Rotate after each action.

11-minute block

  • Round 1 - 2:00 live, 0:30 rest
  • Round 2 - 2:00 live, 0:30 rest
  • Round 3 - 2:00 live, 0:30 rest
  • Round 4 - 2:00 live, 0:30 rest
  • Round 5 - final 1:00 live
Main cue: attack hard, react quickly, and use the speed from the earlier drills to beat the defender or recover.
4

Coaching Notes

Keep the quality high. This session works best when every rep looks sharp and explosive.

For younger athletes

  • Show the visual first, then demo the rep, then let them go.
  • Keep lines short so the athlete stays engaged.
  • Use simple words: quick feet, low hips, push and go.
  • Stop and reset if the athlete loses posture or control.

Best reminders

  • Drive your arms. Strong arm action helps your legs move faster when you accelerate.
  • Get low before the cut. Bend at the hips and knees before you plant so you can push away strongly.
  • Move fast, but stay under control. Keep your feet organized and stay balanced even when working at full speed.
  • Recover for quality. Take enough rest so the next rep is still explosive.
  • Finish by competing. Use the 1v1 to apply your quick feet, acceleration and change of direction against a real opponent.
